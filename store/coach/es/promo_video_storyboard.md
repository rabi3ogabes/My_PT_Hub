## Rules

- App Store previews must be **captured from the app** (screen recordings of the seeded demo tenant), with captions and music added in post. No hands, devices or people in App Store previews; the Play promo may add a 1.5 s hand-held device shot at the start.
- First 3 seconds autoplay muted in both stores: the hook text must work without sound.
- Resolution: 886 × 1920 (iPhone 6.9"), 1080 × 1920 for Play/YouTube; 30 fps; H.264; ≤ 500 MB.
- Poster frame (App Store): the beat 1 frame with the hook text.
- Music: licensed track with worldwide sync rights; no lyrics (localization-neutral).
- Captions use Manrope 800 / IBM Plex Sans Arabic 700, champagne gold `#D4AF6A` on charcoal `#0B0B0F`.
- Demo data only (flagged demo coach "Coach Sara"); never show prices, competitor names or medical claims.
